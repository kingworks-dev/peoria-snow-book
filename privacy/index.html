Privacy

What we keep, and where

Short page, no dark patterns. This guide has no ads, no trackers and no cookie banner, because it does not need one.

Two forms

The monthly email form keeps your first name and email address. The add-a-listing form keeps your name, email, whether it is an addition or a correction, and what you typed. Both are stored as a lead record by KING HOLDINGS VENTURES LLC and used only to answer you or send the letter.

No trackers

There is no analytics script, no ad pixel, no embed from a third party on these pages. Fonts and images are served from this domain. That means no cookie banner, and no third party watching you read.

Email

One letter a month at most. Every letter has a one-line unsubscribe that works without logging in. The address is never sold, rented or given to a business on the list.

Server logs

Our host keeps the ordinary request logs (page, time, browser string) and the form records a hashed IP so repeat spam can be spotted. It is not used to profile anyone.

Removal

Want your email deleted, or your business taken off the list? Send one line and it is done, no questions.
