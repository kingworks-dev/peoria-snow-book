The method

How these Snow plowing and removal are ranked

Short version: we look for businesses that actually serve Peoria, answer the phone, and have a public record behind them. Nobody buys a place on the list.

1. They are really local and really working

A business gets in when it advertises or is listed as serving Peoria or one of the towns around it, and when there is a sign it is still trading: a live website, a working phone, a recent public presence. Defunct pages and lead-farm listings are out.

2. Area covered

Wider genuine coverage ranks higher. A company that works the whole metro is a safer call, especially for the same-week jobs people actually need.

3. Reachability

A published phone number and a real website count for a lot. In this trade, the difference between a good job and a bad week is whether somebody picks up.

4. A public review record

Where a business has a Google listing, the star average and the number of reviews are printed next to it and the review count is weighed. Volume tells you more than a perfect average; a 4.9 from nine reviews is not the same claim as a 4.6 from two hundred.

What a "featured partner" means

Slot No. 01 is a partner of this guide and is labelled as such. That is the whole disclosure: it is not a paid ranking for the other slots, and the partner is one business in the same trade, held to the same criteria.

There is one more thing worth saying plainly — the featured business is owned by King Holdings Ventures LLC, the same company that publishes this guide. That is why the list can stay free and uncluttered. It is also why the label is on the card and the method page rather than buried.

What is printed, and what is not

  • Name, town, phone and website come from the business's own site or its public listing.
  • Ratings and review counts come from Google, read on the date in the page footer. They drift.
  • No invented review quotes, no "since 1998", no crew names unless the business publishes them.
  • Prices are only printed when the business publishes them.

Corrections

Wrong number, closed down, moved town, or you own the business and want the entry fixed? Send a correction and we will change it, including removing an entry on request.
